The MIT and CC BY 4.0 grants in this data/analysis release do not apply to the article or supplementary-material PDF, their LaTeX/BibTeX sources, or editorial attachments. Within this package the exception covers `sections/*.tex` and any other manuscript source file. Complete article PDFs and compilable LaTeX submission archives are distributed separately.

The manuscript excerpts here support comparison with the submitted research. No blanket public reuse license for the manuscript is granted by the package's software or data licenses. Author rights, applicable law and any subsequent publisher agreement or explicit manuscript license govern its reuse and archiving. This notice does not restrict permissions that already arise from law or an applicable separate license.

Standalone plot files in `figures/`, including their PDF exports, remain original figure assets covered by `LICENSE-DATA`; they are distinct from a full article/supplement PDF. This separation does not place the full paper under CC BY or determine the journal publication route.

Identifying author pages, cover letters and signed manuscript files are supplied separately to the editor or retained by the authors.
